## How to use this glossary

- Find the shorthand or phrase from the ticket in the Term column, then search the knowledge base using the wording in the Meaning column. That is the wording our policies, help articles and fee schedule use.
- Use the full term with customers, not the shorthand. Say "Bridge Essentials", not "ESS".
- When a phrase can mean more than one thing, ask before you answer. For example, a "bounced check" can be a check the business wrote (a returned item) or a check it deposited (a deposited item returned), and each has a different fee.
