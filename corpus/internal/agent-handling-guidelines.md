# Agent Handling Guidelines: Fee Credits, Holds and Escalations
Effective: July 1, 2026 · Owner: Contact Center Operations · Internal — agents only

Verify the caller first, as set out in the Caller Identity Verification Procedure, before you discuss fees or holds on a specific account.

## Fee courtesy credits

A courtesy credit reverses a fee that has already posted, as a goodwill gesture; customers often call this waiving the fee. You may reverse one monthly maintenance fee and one returned item (NSF) fee per account per rolling 12 months without supervisor approval, if the account is in good standing. An account is in good standing when it is not in collections and the same fee type has not been reversed in the last 12 months. Rolling 12 months means the 12 months before the request, not the calendar year.

### Before you reverse a fee

Confirm all of the following:

1. The fee is a monthly maintenance fee or a returned item (NSF) fee. No other fee can be reversed without approval.
2. This fee type has not been reversed on the account in the last 12 months.
3. The account is not in collections.
4. The credit is $50 or less.

If any of these is not met, you cannot give the credit on your own.

### When you need supervisor approval

A second reversal of the same fee type within 12 months, or any credit over $50, needs supervisor approval in the case tool. Do not confirm the credit to the customer until the approval is recorded.

### Fees agents can never reverse

Agents can never reverse these fees, even with supervisor approval.

| Fee | Reversal rule |
|---|---|
| Chargeback fee | Never reversible by agents. The chargeback fee is non-refundable regardless of the dispute outcome, even when the merchant wins. |
| PCI compliance program fee | Never reversible by agents. The fee is billed on the account anniversary. |
| PCI non-validation fee | Never reversible by agents. Merchant Risk reverses it only if the SAQ was completed before the fee posted. |
| Card-network arbitration fees | Never reversible by agents. These card-network fees are passed through to the merchant. |
| Terminal non-return fee | Never reversible by agents. Merchant Operations reverses it only if the terminal is received within 15 days of the fee. |

### Record the reason code

Always record the reason code in the case for every fee credit. If a request does not qualify, explain the fee and how to avoid it, such as by meeting the waiver requirements in Business Checking Plans.

## Payout and account holds

### What you may say

When a customer asks about held payouts or a hold on their account, tell them:

> "Your account is under review and our Risk Operations team will contact you by email within 2 business days."

Then create a Risk Ops referral from the case.

### What not to say

- Do not speculate about why funds are held, even if the customer suggests a reason.
- Never mention investigations, suspicious activity reports, or law enforcement.
- Do not promise when or whether held funds will be released.

### Check whether it is standard payout timing

Before you treat a late payout as a hold, check the standard timing in the Payouts and Settlement Policy. Bridge Essentials payouts arrive 2 business days after batch close, and Bridge Plus and Bridge Premier payouts arrive the next business day. Payouts are not initiated on weekends or Federal Reserve holidays, and batches that close on Saturday or Sunday are treated as closing on Monday. A new merchant's first payout may take up to 5 business days while the account is verified.

## Escalation paths

Escalate issues you cannot resolve to the team that owns them:

| Team | Escalate for | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Business Support Tier 2 | Questions the knowledge base does not answer | Escalate rather than guess. Do not tell the customer that MBFS does not offer something unless an article says so. |
| Risk Operations | Payout holds, account holds and reserves | Give the hold statement above and create a Risk Ops referral. |
| Fraud team | Failed caller verification and suspected account takeover | After two failed verification attempts, stop, disclose nothing about the account, and transfer the call. |
| Onboarding | Restricted business types and application status | Do not promise approval to an applicant in a restricted business type. |
| Merchant Operations | Terminals and payouts | Includes terminal non-return fee reversal requests. Send payout holds and reserves to Risk Operations instead. |

Restricted business types are sellers of hemp-derived CBD products, firearms and ammunition dealers, nutraceuticals or dietary supplements sold by subscription, and travel agencies. They may be approved only after enhanced due diligence, so route the applicant to Onboarding. Prohibited business types cannot be approved; the Account Opening and Identity Verification Policy lists them. PCI non-validation fee reversal requests go to Merchant Risk, a different team from Risk Operations.
