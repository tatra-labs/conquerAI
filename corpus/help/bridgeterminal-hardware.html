BridgeTerminal Hardware: Buying, Renting, and Returns

Updated July 1, 2026

MerchantBridge offers two card terminals for in-person sales: the BridgeTerminal Mini and the BridgeTerminal Pro. You can buy either model outright or rent it for a monthly fee. Terminal prices are the same on Bridge Essentials, Bridge Plus, and Bridge Premier.

To order a terminal, sign in to the Merchant Portal or call Business Support at 1-800-555-0142.

Warranty and replacements

  • Purchased terminals are covered by a 1-year warranty.
  • Rented terminals are replaced free of charge if they are defective.

Returning a rented terminal

Only rented terminals need to be returned. A terminal you bought is yours to keep, even if you close your account.

If you cancel a terminal rental or close your MerchantBridge account, return each rented terminal within 30 days of the cancellation or account closure, using the prepaid return label we provide.

  1. Pack the terminal securely, in its original box if you still have it.
  2. Attach the prepaid return label and drop the package off with the carrier shown on the label.
  3. Keep the tracking number until we confirm that we've received the terminal.

Non-return fee: If a rented terminal is not returned within 30 days of cancellation or account closure, a non-return fee is charged for each terminal: $199.00 for a BridgeTerminal Mini or $599.00 for a BridgeTerminal Pro.

If you've lost your return label, call Business Support and we'll send you a new one.

Troubleshooting: restart your terminal

If your terminal freezes, won't connect, or won't complete a sale, restart it:

  1. Make sure the terminal is charged, or connect it to power.
  2. Press and hold the power button until the terminal turns off.
  3. Wait a few seconds, then press the power button again to turn it back on.
  4. Try the sale again.

If the problem continues after a restart:

  • BridgeTerminal Mini won't connect: make sure Bluetooth is on and the MerchantBridge app is open and up to date on your phone. If the Mini still won't connect, remove it from the app's paired devices and pair it again.
  • BridgeTerminal Pro is offline: check that the Pro is connected to Wi-Fi or LTE. If you've changed your Wi-Fi network or password, reconnect the Pro to Wi-Fi.
  • Pro receipts won't print: open the printer cover and check that the paper roll is loaded correctly.

Still having trouble? Call Business Support at 1-800-555-0142. If the terminal is defective, we'll replace a rented terminal free of charge, and a purchased terminal is covered by its 1-year warranty.
