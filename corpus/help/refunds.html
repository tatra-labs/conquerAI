Issuing Refunds to Customers

Updated April 20, 2026

You can refund a card sale in full or in part from the Merchant Portal or from your BridgeTerminal. The refund goes back to the card your customer used for the original purchase.

Before you refund

  • You can issue a refund within 120 days of the original sale.
  • You can't refund more than the original sale amount. Partial refunds count toward that total.
  • Processing fees from the original sale are not returned when you issue a refund.
  • Refunds are deducted from your next payout. If your next payout isn't enough to cover a refund, the remaining amount is deducted from your MerchantBridge business checking balance.

Refund a sale in the Merchant Portal

  1. Sign in to the Merchant Portal at portal.merchantbridge.example.
  2. Go to Transactions and open the original sale.
  3. Select Refund, then choose Full refund or Partial refund.
  4. For a partial refund, enter the amount you want to refund.
  5. Review the amount, then select Confirm refund.

Refund a sale on your BridgeTerminal

  1. Open the transaction history and select the sale. If you use a BridgeTerminal Mini, do this in the MerchantBridge app on the phone paired with your Mini.
  2. Tap Refund, then choose the full amount or enter a partial amount.
  3. Confirm the refund. On a BridgeTerminal Pro, you can print a refund receipt for your customer.

When your customer sees the refund

Customers usually see a refund on their card account in 5–10 business days, depending on their card issuer. If a customer asks where their refund is, share the refund date and amount from the Merchant Portal so they can follow up with their issuer.

Avoid cash refunds for card sales

If a customer paid by card, refund the card instead of handing back cash. A cash refund doesn't appear on the customer's card account, so the customer could still dispute the original charge with their card issuer. If that happens, you could face a chargeback and lose the sale amount a second time, plus a chargeback fee.

Common questions

Can I refund a sale that's more than 120 days old?

No. Refunds can be issued only within 120 days of the original sale. If you need help with an older sale, call Business Support at 1-800-555-0142.

Why did a refund come out of my checking account?

Refunds are deducted from your next payout first. If that payout isn't large enough to cover the refund, the rest is deducted from your MerchantBridge business checking balance.
