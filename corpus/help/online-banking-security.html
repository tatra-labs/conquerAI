Online Banking Sign-in and Security

Updated March 12, 2026

Your MerchantBridge sign-in protects your business accounts and your payouts. Here's how two-step verification, password resets, and automatic sign-out work, and how to spot and report scams.

Two-step verification

Two-step verification (sometimes called two-factor authentication or 2FA) is required for every user. When you sign in, you enter your password and then a one-time passcode from either:

  • an authenticator app on your phone, or
  • a text message (SMS) sent to your mobile number.

You can switch between an authenticator app and text messages in Settings. We recommend an authenticator app, because text messages can be redirected if someone takes over your mobile number. If you lose the phone you use for passcodes, call Business Support at 1-800-555-0142.

Reset your password

  1. On the sign-in page, select Forgot password.
  2. Enter the verification code we send you to confirm it's you.
  3. Create a new password that you don't use for any other account.

If you get a verification code you didn't request, don't share it with anyone, and change your password. Someone may be trying to sign in as you.

Automatic sign-out

For your security, sessions time out after 15 minutes of inactivity, and you'll need to sign in again. Submit any payments or changes before you step away, and always sign out on a shared computer.

What MerchantBridge will never ask for

MerchantBridge will never call, text, or email you to ask for your one-time passcode, password, PIN, or full card number. If anyone asks for them, don't share them: end the call or ignore the message, and call the 24/7 Card Fraud line at 1-800-555-0199 or Business Support at 1-800-555-0142.

When you call Business Support, we may send a "Verify this call" prompt to your MerchantBridge app. Approve it only if you called us and are on the line with an agent.

Spot and report phishing

Be cautious with any email, text, or call that:

  • pressures you to act right away to avoid a locked account or a missed payment,
  • includes a link to a sign-in page, or
  • asks you to change where payments or payouts are sent.

Report phishing emails and texts to phishing@merchantbridge.example, then delete them. If you entered your password or a passcode on a suspicious site, reset your password right away and call Business Support. If you shared card details, lock your card in the app (Cards > Lock card) and call the 24/7 Card Fraud line at 1-800-555-0199.

If a vendor emails you new or changed payment instructions, confirm them by calling a phone number you already know, not one from the email.

Add users instead of sharing a sign-in

Give everyone who needs access their own sign-in. An owner or admin can add users in Settings, and each new user sets up their own password and two-step verification. When someone leaves your business, an owner or admin should remove their access right away.

For your protection, changes to users, your payout bank account, or your contact email or phone number must be approved in the MerchantBridge app by an owner or admin. Our team can't make these changes for you over the phone.

Common questions

Can I turn off two-step verification?

No. Two-step verification is required for every user and can't be turned off.

Why was I signed out?

Sessions time out after 15 minutes of inactivity. Sign in again to continue.
