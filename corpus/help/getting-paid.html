Getting Paid: Payout Timing and Instant Payouts

Updated July 5, 2026

When you accept card payments with MerchantBridge, your sales are grouped into a daily batch and paid out on a schedule set by your business checking plan. If you need funds sooner, you can request an Instant Payout to an eligible debit card at any time.

How standard payouts work

Card transactions are grouped into a daily batch that closes automatically at 8:00 PM ET. Sales made after 8:00 PM ET are included in the next day's batch. Once a batch closes, your payout arrives on the timeline for your plan.

Plan Standard payout timing Batch closes Friday Batch closes Saturday or Sunday
Bridge Essentials 2 business days after the batch closes Arrives Tuesday Arrives Wednesday
Bridge Plus Next business day after the batch closes Arrives Monday Arrives Tuesday
Bridge Premier Next business day after the batch closes Arrives Monday Arrives Tuesday

Examples assume a week with no Federal Reserve holidays.

Weekends and holidays

Standard payouts are not initiated on weekends or Federal Reserve holidays. Batches that close on Saturday or Sunday are treated as closing on Monday, so payouts for weekend sales arrive on Wednesday on Bridge Essentials and on Tuesday on Bridge Plus and Bridge Premier. Federal Reserve holidays are not business days, so they're skipped when counting business days to your payout. Instant Payouts are still available on weekends and holidays.

Your first payout

If you're new to MerchantBridge, your first payout may take up to 5 business days while we verify your account. After that, payouts follow your plan's standard timing.

Instant Payouts

Need your money the same day, or over a weekend? Instant Payouts are available 24/7 and send funds to an eligible debit card in your business's name. Instant Payouts usually arrive within 30 minutes.

How to request an Instant Payout

  1. Sign in to the MerchantBridge app or the Merchant Portal and go to Payouts.
  2. Select Instant Payout.
  3. Choose the debit card that should receive the funds. It must be an eligible debit card in your business's name. If you haven't added one yet, select Add debit card and follow the prompts. Adding or changing an Instant Payout debit card must be approved in the app by an owner or admin.
  4. Enter the amount you want to send, up to $10,000.
  5. Review the fee shown on screen, then select Confirm.

Instant Payout fees and limits

The fee for each Instant Payout depends on your plan:

  • Bridge Essentials and Bridge Plus: Instant Payouts cost 1.5% of the payout amount, with a $0.50 minimum.
  • Bridge Premier: Instant Payouts cost 1.0% of the payout amount, with a $0.50 minimum.

Instant Payouts cost 1.25% of the payout amount, with no minimum.

The $0.50 minimum applies whenever the percentage fee would be less than $0.50. You can send up to $10,000 per Instant Payout and up to $25,000 per day.

Why a payout may be lower or held

  • Refunds you issue are deducted from your next payout or your checking balance.
  • When we receive a chargeback, we debit the disputed amount plus the chargeback fee from your next payout, or from your checking account if your payouts aren't enough to cover it.
  • If we see elevated chargeback or refund risk, we may hold payouts or set a rolling reserve of up to 10% of daily card volume, held for up to 90 days.

Switching plans

Your plan sets both your standard payout timing and your Instant Payout fee. You can switch plans at any time in Settings > Plan. Switching is free, and your new plan's fees and payout timing take effect at the start of your next statement cycle.
