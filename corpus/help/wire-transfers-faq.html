Wire Transfers FAQ

Updated February 10, 2025

Quick answers to common questions about sending wire transfers from your MerchantBridge business checking account in Online Banking or the MerchantBridge app.

What's the cut-off time to send a domestic wire the same day?

Domestic wires submitted and approved by 3:00 PM ET on a business day are sent the same day. If your business uses dual approval, the second approval must also be completed by the cut-off. Wires submitted or approved after 3:00 PM ET are sent the next business day.

What time is the cut-off for international wires?

International wires must be submitted and approved by 1:00 PM ET on a business day to be sent that day. International wires submitted or approved after 1:00 PM ET are sent the next business day.

How long does it take for a wire to arrive?

Domestic wires usually arrive the same business day. International wires usually take 1–3 business days, and can take longer if intermediary banks are involved.

How much does it cost to send an international wire?

The fee for each outgoing international wire is $45 on Essentials, $40 on Plus, and $25 on Premier. Intermediary or correspondent banks may also deduct their own fees along the way, so your recipient may receive less than the amount you sent. MerchantBridge doesn't control fees deducted by intermediary or beneficiary banks.

What information do I need to send a wire?

You'll need the beneficiary's name and address, their account number (or IBAN, where applicable), the name of their bank, and the bank's ABA routing number for a domestic wire or its SWIFT/BIC code for an international wire. Some countries also require a purpose of payment.

Is there a daily limit on how much I can wire?

Daily outgoing wire limits depend on your plan: $25,000 on Essentials, $100,000 on Plus, and $250,000 on Premier. If you need a higher limit, call Business Support at 1-800-555-0142 to request an increase.

A vendor emailed me new wire instructions. Is it safe to use them?

Verify new or changed wire instructions before you send money: call the vendor at a phone number you already know, never one taken from the email. Scammers often pose as vendors or executives and email fake payment instructions, a scam known as business email compromise. MerchantBridge will never ask you to send a wire to "test" or "verify" an account.

Can I cancel a wire after I send it?

Wires are final once they're released, so they can't be canceled. If you sent a wire by mistake, call Business Support at 1-800-555-0142 as soon as possible. We can request a recall from the receiving bank for a $35 fee per request, but the return of funds isn't guaranteed: it depends on the beneficiary bank, and the fee is charged whether or not the recall succeeds.

Still have a question about a wire? Call Business Support at 1-800-555-0142 or send us a secure message in the app or Merchant Portal.
