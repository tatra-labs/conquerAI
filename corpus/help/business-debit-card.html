Using Your Bridge Business Debit Mastercard

Updated July 1, 2026

Your Bridge Business Debit Mastercard draws directly from your MerchantBridge business checking account. Use it anywhere Mastercard is accepted: in person, online, or at an ATM. Here's what to know about ATM fees, using your card outside the U.S., daily limits, and employee cards.

ATM withdrawals

  • Allpoint ATMs: Withdraw cash surcharge-free at any ATM in the Allpoint network. Allpoint ATMs are in our network, so our out-of-network ATM fee doesn't apply there. To find one nearby, open the MerchantBridge app and go to Cards > Find an ATM.
  • Other ATMs: Withdrawals at ATMs outside the Allpoint network have a $2.50 out-of-network ATM fee, plus any surcharge the ATM operator charges.
  • Premier rebates: On Bridge Premier, we rebate up to 4 out-of-network ATM fees per statement cycle.

Using your card outside the U.S.

On Bridge Essentials and Bridge Plus, a foreign transaction fee of 1% of the transaction applies to purchases and ATM withdrawals processed outside the U.S. Bridge Premier has no foreign transaction fee. Online purchases from merchants based outside the U.S. may also be processed abroad, so the foreign transaction fee can apply to those purchases too.

Daily limits and card fees by plan

The daily ATM withdrawal limit is $1,000 on every plan. Your daily purchase limit depends on your plan: $10,000 on Bridge Essentials and Bridge Plus, and $25,000 on Bridge Premier.

Limit or fee Bridge Essentials Bridge Plus Bridge Premier
Daily purchase limit $10,000 $10,000 $25,000
Daily ATM withdrawal limit $1,000 $1,000 $1,000
Out-of-network ATM fee (per withdrawal) $2.50 $2.50 $2.50, up to 4 rebated per statement cycle
Foreign transaction fee 1% of the transaction 1% of the transaction None

Employee cards

Owners and admins can order up to 10 employee cards with spend controls. Employee cards draw from your business checking account, and you decide how each one can be used:

  • Set a spending limit for each employee card.
  • Lock or unlock any employee card at any time.
  • Employees can lock their own card, request a replacement, and see recent transactions on their card. They can't see account balances.

To order an employee card or change its spend controls, go to Cards > Employee cards in the MerchantBridge app.

Lock or report your card

If you can't find your card, lock it instantly in the MerchantBridge app under Cards > Lock card. Unlocking the card restores it.

If your card is lost, stolen, or compromised, report it right away in the app under Cards > Report a problem, or call the 24/7 Card Fraud line at 1-800-555-0199. Reporting a card cancels it and issues a new card number. Standard replacement (5–7 business days) is free; expedited replacement (2 business days) is $25, or free on Bridge Premier. For step-by-step help, see Lost, Stolen, or Compromised Debit Card.

MerchantBridge will never call, text, or email you to ask for your PIN, full card number, password, or one-time passcode.
