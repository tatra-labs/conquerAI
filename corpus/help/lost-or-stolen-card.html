Lost, Stolen, or Compromised Debit Card

Updated February 10, 2026

If your Bridge Business Debit Mastercard is lost or stolen, or you think someone has your card details, act right away. The steps below help protect your business account and get a replacement card on its way.

What to do right away

  1. Lock your card in the app. Go to Cards > Lock card. Locking takes effect instantly. If you find the card before you report it, unlock it to restore it.
  2. Report the card. Report it in the app under Cards > Report a problem, or call our 24/7 Card Fraud line at 1-800-555-0199. Reporting cancels the card and issues a new card number.
  3. Review your recent transactions. Look through recent activity on the card for any charges you don't recognize.
  4. Dispute unauthorized transactions. Dispute any transaction you didn't authorize within 60 days of the statement date on which it first appeared. Provisional credit for eligible claims is typically issued within 10 business days.

These timelines are MerchantBridge policy. Federal Regulation E consumer protections do not apply to business accounts. Mastercard Zero Liability protection applies to unauthorized purchases reported promptly (conditions apply).

Not sure whether the card is lost or just misplaced? Lock it first. Reporting can't be undone: a reported card is canceled and can't be used again.

If an employee loses their card, they can lock and replace their own card in the app. Owners and admins can also manage any card on the account.

Your replacement card

When you report a card, we send a replacement with a new card number. You can choose how quickly it ships:

  • Standard replacement (5–7 business days): free on all plans.
  • Expedited replacement (2 business days): $25.00 on Bridge Essentials and Bridge Plus; free on Bridge Premier.

Because your card number changes, update any subscriptions, recurring payments, or saved card details that used the old card.

Use your new card before it arrives

You can add your new card to Apple Pay or Google Pay right away, before the plastic card arrives in the mail. In the app, go to Cards, select the new card, and choose Add to digital wallet.

How to spot card skimming

Skimmers are devices that criminals attach to card readers to copy card information. They are most often found at gas station pumps and standalone ATMs. Before you use your card at one, take a moment to check for:

  • A card slot that looks loose, bulky, crooked, or different from the reader on the next pump or machine. Give it a gentle wiggle; a genuine reader shouldn't move.
  • A keypad that feels thick or spongy, or sits higher than the surface around it.
  • A broken, peeled, or missing security seal on the pump's cabinet panel.
  • Pinholes or small add-on pieces near the keypad that could hold a camera.

Prefer tap-to-pay when it's available, so your card never goes into the card slot. Cover the keypad with your other hand when you enter your PIN. If you suspect your card was skimmed, lock it, report it, and request a replacement.

Protect yourself from scams

We will never ask for your PIN, full card number, or one-time passcode. We also never call, text, or email to ask for your password. If someone contacts you claiming to be from MerchantBridge and asks for this information, hang up and call the 24/7 Card Fraud line at 1-800-555-0199. You can forward suspicious emails to phishing@merchantbridge.example.
