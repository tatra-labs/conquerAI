Depositing Checks with Mobile Deposit

Updated August 1, 2025

Mobile deposit lets you deposit checks made payable to your business from anywhere, using the MerchantBridge app. Here's how to make a deposit, when your money will be available, and what to do if a check is rejected.

How to deposit a check

  1. Endorse the check. On the back, sign your name and write "For mobile deposit only at MerchantBridge".
  2. Open the app. Sign in to the MerchantBridge app and select Deposit a check.
  3. Enter the details. Choose the account to deposit to, and enter the check amount exactly as it's written on the check.
  4. Take photos. Place the check on a dark, flat surface in good light and photograph the front and back. Make sure all four corners are in the frame and the image is in focus.
  5. Submit. Review the details and select Submit. You'll see a confirmation in the app once your deposit is received.

When your money is available

For checks deposited by 7:00 PM ET on a business day, the first $275 is available the next business day, and the rest is available by the second business day. Deposits made after 7:00 PM ET, or on a weekend or holiday, are treated as made on the next business day.

For example, if you deposit a check by 7:00 PM ET on a Monday with no holiday that week, the first $275 is available on Tuesday and the rest by Wednesday.

In some cases we may hold funds longer, for up to 7 business days. This can happen, for example, when deposits total more than $6,725 in one day, for new accounts in their first 30 days, when a check is being redeposited, or when there's reason to doubt a check will be paid. If we place a hold, we'll let you know in the app and by email.

Mobile deposit limits

Mobile deposit limits depend on your business checking plan.

Plan Daily limit (per business day) Limit per statement cycle
Bridge Essentials $5,000 $20,000
Bridge Plus $10,000 $50,000
Bridge Premier $25,000 $150,000

Common reasons a deposit is rejected

  • The photos are blurry, too dark, or cut off, or not all four corners of the check are visible.
  • The endorsement is missing or doesn't include "For mobile deposit only at MerchantBridge".
  • The amount you entered doesn't match the amount written on the check.
  • The deposit would go over your daily or statement-cycle limit.
  • The check has already been deposited.
  • The check is damaged or altered, or the numbers along the bottom of the check can't be read.

Most issues can be fixed by retaking the photos or correcting the endorsement or amount, then submitting the check again. If you need help, call Business Support at 1-800-555-0142.

After you deposit

Once your deposit is submitted, keep the paper check for 30 days in a secure place, then destroy it, for example by shredding it. Don't deposit the same check again, whether through mobile deposit or any other way.

If a check you deposited is returned unpaid, the deposit is reversed and a deposited item returned fee of $12.00 applies.
