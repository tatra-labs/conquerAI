Tax Forms: 1099-K and 1099-INT

Updated January 8, 2026

Each year, MerchantBridge Financial Services (MBFS) issues tax forms that report the card payments you received through us and Bridge Reserve Savings interest of $10 or more. Here's which forms to expect, what's on them, and where to find them.

Will I receive a Form 1099-K?

You'll receive a Form 1099-K if you received card payments through MerchantBridge during the year. MerchantBridge is the merchant acquirer for your card payments (the company that processes them and pays them out to you), so we issue Form 1099-K to every merchant that received card payments during the year. There is no minimum dollar amount or number of transactions for card payments.

Your 1099-K shows the gross amount of your card payments for the year, before refunds, chargebacks, and processing fees are subtracted, so it may not match the payouts deposited to your account.

Do the $20,000 and 200-transaction reporting thresholds apply to my card payments?

You may have seen 1099-K reporting thresholds such as $20,000 and 200 transactions mentioned in the news or by other payment services. Those thresholds apply to third-party settlement organizations (TPSOs), such as some payment apps and online marketplaces. The TPSO thresholds don't apply to card payments processed by a merchant acquirer like MerchantBridge, so you'll receive a Form 1099-K for your card payments whatever your total for the year.

What is backup withholding?

Your 1099-K is reported under the taxpayer identification number (TIN) on your account: your EIN, or your SSN if you're a sole proprietor who uses one. If the name and TIN on your account don't match IRS records, MBFS may be required to apply 24% backup withholding to your payouts, after notifying you, until the mismatch is resolved. Backup withholding amounts are sent to the IRS and shown on your Form 1099-K.

To avoid backup withholding:

  1. Make sure the legal name and TIN on your account exactly match what the IRS has on file for your business.
  2. If we notify you of a mismatch, submit a corrected Form W-9 in the Merchant Portal under Settings > Tax information. Backup withholding stops once we receive your corrected W-9.

Will I receive a Form 1099-INT?

You'll receive a Form 1099-INT if your Bridge Reserve Savings account earned $10 or more in interest during the year. Form 1099-INT shows the interest we paid you. Bridge business checking accounts don't earn interest, so they don't generate a Form 1099-INT.

When and where can I get my tax forms?

  • Online: Your Form 1099-K and Form 1099-INT for the previous tax year are available in the Merchant Portal by January 31. Go to Documents > Tax forms to view or download them.
  • By mail: To have a paper copy mailed to the address on file, call Business Support at 1-800-555-0142 or send us a secure message in the app or Merchant Portal.

Can MerchantBridge give me tax advice?

We're happy to help you find your forms and explain what they show. MBFS cannot provide tax advice. Please consult a qualified tax professional.
