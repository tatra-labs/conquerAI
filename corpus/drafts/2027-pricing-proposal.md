# 2027 Pricing Proposal (Draft)
DRAFT — not approved · Owner: Pricing · Prepared: August 2026

> **DRAFT — not approved, pending Pricing Committee review — do not share with customers**
>
> The proposed amounts below are not approved and must not be quoted to customers. Current fees are in the Business Fee & Rate Schedule (effective July 1, 2026).

## Purpose

This proposal recommends four changes to the Business Fee & Rate Schedule for 2027, for discussion at Pricing Committee review. If the proposal is approved, the Committee would set the effective date.

## Proposed changes

| Code | Service | Plan | Current fee | Proposed fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MER-05 | Chargeback | All plans | $25.00 | $35.00 |
| ACC-01 | Monthly maintenance fee | Bridge Essentials | $12.00 | $16.00 |
| MER-04 | Instant Payout | Bridge Essentials and Bridge Plus | 1.5% of payout (min $0.50) | 2.0% of payout (min $0.50) |
| WIR-01 | Domestic wire – outgoing | Bridge Essentials | $30.00 | $35.00 |

## Rationale

### Chargeback fee (MER-05)

- Each chargeback requires notifying the merchant, collecting evidence through the Merchant Portal, and submitting a response to the card issuer. The current fee does not fully recover that handling work.
- A higher fee strengthens the incentive for merchants to resolve problems directly with their customers, for example through a refund, before a cardholder disputes the charge.
- The fee structure would not change: the fee would remain non-refundable regardless of the dispute outcome, and card-network arbitration fees would still be passed through separately.

### Bridge Essentials monthly maintenance fee (ACC-01)

- Essentials has the lowest monthly fee and the lowest waiver thresholds of the three plans, and the cost of servicing the plan has grown.
- No change is proposed to the waiver requirements ($2,500 average daily balance, or at least $1,000 in card-processing deposits during the statement cycle), so customers who meet the waiver would still pay no monthly fee.
- The increase narrows the gap to Bridge Plus, which includes more transactions per statement cycle and next-business-day payouts, and encourages growing businesses to upgrade.

### Instant Payout (MER-04)

- Instant Payouts are available 24/7 and usually arrive within 30 minutes. MBFS funds them ahead of standard settlement and covers the cost of pushing funds to the merchant's debit card.
- Standard payouts would be unaffected, so merchants who do not need immediate access to funds would see no change.
- No change is proposed for Bridge Premier.

### Domestic outgoing wire on Bridge Essentials (WIR-01)

- Outgoing wires are final once released, so each one carries fraud screening and verification work, including controls against business email compromise.
- The increase widens the price difference between Essentials and Bridge Plus ($25.00) and Bridge Premier (included), supporting upgrades for businesses that send wires regularly.

## Customer impact and communication

- Bridge Essentials customers who do not meet the waiver and regularly use Instant Payouts or domestic outgoing wires would see the largest combined increase.
- Any approved change requires a customer notice, emailed at least 30 days before the effective date, and an updated Business Fee & Rate Schedule.
- Contact-center guidance and help articles would need updating before the effective date.

## Open questions for the Pricing Committee

1. What effective date to set, and whether to introduce the changes together or in stages.
2. Whether the chargeback fee increase should apply equally to all plans.
3. Whether a smaller Instant Payout increase would meet the cost-recovery goal with less customer impact.
4. Expected attrition among Bridge Essentials customers who do not meet the waiver.
