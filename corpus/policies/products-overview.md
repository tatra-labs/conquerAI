# MerchantBridge Products and Services
Effective: July 1, 2026 · Owner: Product

MerchantBridge Financial Services (MBFS) provides business banking and merchant card processing for small and mid-sized businesses. Accounts are available to US-registered sole proprietors, LLCs, corporations, partnerships and nonprofits with a US physical address. Some business types can't be approved; see the Account Opening and Identity Verification Policy.

## What we offer

- **Business checking.** Three plans: Bridge Essentials, Bridge Plus and Bridge Premier. Every plan includes Online Banking, the MBFS app, mobile deposit and the Bridge Business Debit Mastercard. The plans differ in fee waiver requirements, included transactions and payout timing; see Business Checking Plans.
- **Bridge Reserve Savings.** An interest-bearing business savings account with tiered, variable rates. It must be linked to an MBFS business checking account; business checking accounts themselves do not earn interest.
- **Merchant card processing.** Accept cards in person (tap, dip or swipe) and online, by invoice or by keyed entry. Card sales are paid out on your plan's standard schedule or, for a fee, usually within 30 minutes with an Instant Payout to an eligible debit card in your business's name. Manage refunds and disputes in the Merchant Portal (portal.merchantbridge.example), and complete your annual PCI Self-Assessment Questionnaire in BridgeSecure.
- **BridgeTerminal hardware.** Choose the BridgeTerminal Mini, a Bluetooth reader that pairs with the MBFS app on your phone, or the BridgeTerminal Pro, with a built-in receipt printer, Wi-Fi and LTE, tipping and a PIN pad. Both can be purchased or rented.
- **Bridge Business Debit Mastercard.** Use it for purchases and ATM withdrawals, and add up to 10 employee cards with spend controls.
- **ACH payments and wire transfers.** Send standard and same-day ACH payments and domestic and international wires from Online Banking or the MBFS app, and receive incoming wires.

## What we don't offer

MBFS does not offer the following products:

- **Personal or consumer accounts.** Every MBFS account is a business account, including accounts for sole proprietors.
- **Loans or lines of credit.**
- **Credit cards.** The Bridge Business Debit Mastercard is a debit card: purchases are paid from your business checking balance.
- **Overdraft coverage.** Payments presented without enough funds in your account are returned unpaid, and a returned item (NSF) fee applies to each one, up to 3 fees per business day. See the NSF fee in the Business Fee & Rate Schedule.

## Your deposits and FDIC insurance

MerchantBridge is a financial technology company, not a bank. Banking services are provided by Lanternstone Bank, N.A., Member FDIC, and deposits in your MBFS business checking and Bridge Reserve Savings accounts are held at Lanternstone Bank.

- FDIC insurance covers up to $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, for each account ownership category.
- FDIC insurance protects against the failure of Lanternstone Bank, not the failure of MerchantBridge.
- Checking and Bridge Reserve Savings balances held by the same business at Lanternstone Bank are added together toward the $250,000 limit.
- For a sole proprietor, business deposits are combined with the owner's personal single-ownership accounts at Lanternstone Bank toward the same $250,000 limit.
- Balances above the $250,000 limit are not insured.
