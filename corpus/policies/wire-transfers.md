# Wire Transfer Policy
Effective: March 1, 2026 · Owner: Payments Operations

This policy covers domestic and international wire transfers to and from your MerchantBridge business checking account: cut-off times, delivery timing, the information you need, how to protect your business from wire fraud, recalls, limits, and foreign currency.

## Wire cut-off times

Wires are processed on business days. To go out the same business day, a wire must be submitted **and approved** in Online Banking or the MerchantBridge app by:

- **Domestic wires:** 4:00 PM ET
- **International wires:** 2:00 PM ET

If your business uses dual approval, the second approval must also be completed before the cut-off. Wires submitted or approved after the cut-off are processed on the next business day.

## Wire delivery timing

- **Domestic wires** usually arrive the same business day.
- **International wires** usually arrive in 1–3 business days, and can take longer when intermediary banks are involved.

Intermediary or correspondent banks may deduct their own fees as an international wire passes through them, so the amount that arrives may be less than the amount sent. This applies to both outgoing and incoming international wires. MerchantBridge does not control fees deducted by intermediary or beneficiary banks.

Incoming wires are available in your account the same business day we receive them.

## Information you need to send a wire

- Beneficiary's name and address
- Beneficiary's account number (IBAN, where applicable)
- Beneficiary bank's name
- ABA routing number (domestic wires) or SWIFT/BIC code (international wires)
- Purpose of payment, which some countries require

Check every detail before you approve the wire.

## Protect yourself from business email compromise

In business email compromise (BEC), a fraudster poses as a vendor, customer, or executive — often from a hacked or look-alike email address — and asks you to send a wire or to change where payments go.

- **Verify new or changed wire instructions by phone.** Call a phone number you already know, such as one from a contract or an earlier invoice — never one provided in the message asking for the change. Never rely on email alone.
- **Slow down when a request is urgent or confidential.** Pressure to act quickly or to keep a payment quiet is a common warning sign.
- **Use dual approval** so a second authorized user reviews each wire before it's sent.

> **Important:** MerchantBridge will never ask you to send a wire to "test" or "verify" an account. If you get a request like this, don't send any money, and report the message to phishing@merchantbridge.example.

## Wire recalls, amendments, and traces

Wires are final once released, so a wire can't be canceled after it's sent. If a wire was sent in error or because of fraud, MerchantBridge can request a recall from the receiving bank, but the return of funds is not guaranteed and depends on the beneficiary bank. Acting quickly gives the best chance of recovery, so call Business Support at 1-800-555-0142 as soon as possible (Mon–Fri 7:00 AM–10:00 PM ET, Sat 9:00 AM–6:00 PM ET, closed Sunday).

Business Support can also request an amendment to correct the details of a wire that has already been sent, or a trace to locate a wire that hasn't arrived.

Each recall, amendment, or trace request costs **$35.00** (fee code WIR-05 in the Business Fee & Rate Schedule), charged whether or not the recall is successful.

## Daily outgoing wire limits

| Plan | Daily outgoing wire limit |
|---|---|
| Bridge Essentials | $25,000 |
| Bridge Plus | $100,000 |
| Bridge Premier | $250,000 |

If you need a higher limit, you can request an increase through Business Support. Requests are subject to review.

## Foreign currency wires

- Incoming wires in a foreign currency are converted to U.S. dollars at MerchantBridge's exchange rate on the day the wire is received.
- Outgoing international wires can be sent in U.S. dollars or in a foreign currency.
- Our exchange rate includes a margin.

## Wire fees

Wire fees depend on your plan and on whether a wire is domestic or international, incoming or outgoing. For current amounts, see the Business Fee & Rate Schedule (fee codes WIR-01 through WIR-05).
