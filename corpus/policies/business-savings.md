# Bridge Reserve Savings Account Terms
Effective: July 1, 2026 · Owner: Deposit Products

Bridge Reserve Savings is an interest-bearing savings account for MerchantBridge business customers. These terms explain who can open an account, how rates and interest work, and which fees apply.

## Eligibility

A Bridge Reserve Savings account must be linked to an MBFS business checking account on Bridge Essentials, Bridge Plus or Bridge Premier. Bridge business checking accounts do not earn interest, so Bridge Reserve Savings is the way to earn interest on balances your business doesn't need day to day.

## Interest rates

Bridge Reserve Savings pays tiered, variable interest rates. Rates may change at any time after the account is opened. Current rates are published on the Bridge Reserve Savings Rate Sheet; the rates below are from the Rate Sheet effective October 1, 2026.

| Tier | End-of-day balance | Interest rate | APY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $0.00 – $9,999.99 | 0.50% | 0.50% |
| 2 | $10,000.00 – $49,999.99 | 2.23% | 2.25% |
| 3 | $50,000.00 – $249,999.99 | 3.05% | 3.10% |
| 4 | $250,000.00 and above | 3.34% | 3.40% |

APY means annual percentage yield. Fees may reduce earnings.

## How interest is calculated

- Interest is compounded daily and credited to your account monthly.
- The tier that your end-of-day balance falls into determines the rate for your entire balance. Rates are not blended: your whole balance earns the rate of one tier.
- Because the tier is based on each day's end-of-day balance, the rate your balance earns changes when your balance moves into a different tier.

> **Example:** Your Bridge Reserve Savings balance at the end of the day is $60,000. That balance falls in Tier 3 ($50,000.00 – $249,999.99), so the Tier 3 rate of 3.05% (3.10% APY) applies to the entire $60,000, not just to the portion above $50,000. No part of the balance earns the lower Tier 1 or Tier 2 rates.

## Withdrawals

You can make 6 withdrawals from Bridge Reserve Savings per statement cycle at no charge. Each withdrawal beyond 6 in the same statement cycle is charged $10.00 (EXCESS WITHDRAWAL FEE on your statement).

## Monthly fee

A $5.00 monthly maintenance fee (SAVINGS MAINT FEE on your statement) applies unless you keep a minimum daily balance of $1,000. Your minimum daily balance is the lowest end-of-day balance during the statement cycle, so the fee is waived only when your balance ends every day of the cycle at $1,000 or more. Business checking plan waivers use average daily balance instead; see Business Checking Plans.

## Tax reporting

If your Bridge Reserve Savings account earns $10 or more in interest during the tax year, MBFS issues Form 1099-INT. Tax forms are available in the Merchant Portal by January 31. MBFS cannot provide tax advice. Please consult a qualified tax professional.

## Deposit insurance

Bridge Reserve Savings deposits are held at Lanternstone Bank, N.A., Member FDIC. Your Bridge Reserve Savings and business checking balances are added together toward the FDIC insurance limit; see MerchantBridge Products and Services for details.
