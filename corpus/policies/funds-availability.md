# Funds Availability Policy
Effective: July 1, 2025 · Owner: Deposit Operations

This policy explains when money you deposit into your MerchantBridge business account is available to spend, transfer, or withdraw, and the rules that apply to mobile check deposits.

## Business days and the check deposit cut-off

A business day is Monday through Friday, excluding Federal Reserve holidays. A check deposit made by 7:00 PM ET on a business day is considered received that day. A check deposited after 7:00 PM ET, or on a Saturday, Sunday, or holiday, is considered received on the next business day.

## When deposited funds are available

- **Electronic deposits**, including ACH credits and incoming wires: available the same business day.
- **Check deposits**, including mobile deposits, made by 7:00 PM ET on a business day: the first $275 is available the next business day, and the rest by the second business day.

For example, if you deposit a check by 7:00 PM ET on a Monday, the first $275 is available on Tuesday and the rest by Wednesday. A check deposited on a Saturday is considered received on Monday, so the same Tuesday and Wednesday timing applies. These examples assume no holidays.

Payouts from your card sales follow the timing in the Payouts and Settlement Policy.

## Longer holds on check deposits (exception holds)

In some situations, we may hold funds from a check deposit for up to 7 business days. We may place an exception hold when:

- Your check deposits total more than $6,725 in one day.
- Your account is new, during its first 30 days.
- You redeposit a check that was returned unpaid.
- Your account has had repeated overdrafts (a negative balance on multiple occasions).
- We have reasonable doubt that a check will be paid (collectability).

If we place an exception hold, we notify you in the app and by email and tell you when the funds will be available.

> **Note:** Available funds are not a guarantee that a check has been paid. If a check you deposited is returned unpaid, we deduct its amount from your account, and the deposited item returned fee in our Fee & Rate Schedule applies.

## Mobile deposit limits

Mobile deposit limits depend on your business checking plan and apply to the total of all checks you deposit with mobile deposit.

| Plan | Mobile deposit limit per business day | Mobile deposit limit per statement cycle |
|---|---|---|
| Bridge Essentials | $5,000 | $20,000 |
| Bridge Plus | $10,000 | $50,000 |
| Bridge Premier | $25,000 | $150,000 |

## Endorsing a check for mobile deposit

Before you photograph the check, endorse the back with:

1. Your signature.
2. The words "For mobile deposit only at MerchantBridge".

A check without this endorsement may be rejected.

## Keeping the paper check after a mobile deposit

Keep the paper check in a secure place for 14 days after your mobile deposit, in case we need to see it. After 14 days, destroy it, for example by shredding it. Do not deposit the same check again, with us or at another bank.
