# Account Opening and Identity Verification Policy
Effective: February 1, 2026 · Owner: Compliance

This policy explains who can open a MerchantBridge business account, the information and documents we need, how long a decision takes, and which businesses we cannot serve or can serve only after enhanced review.

Federal law requires financial institutions to obtain, verify, and record information that identifies each person who opens an account. We apply these requirements to every business we serve and to certain individuals who own or control it.

## Who can apply

You can apply if your business is registered in the United States and is one of the following:

- Sole proprietorship
- Limited liability company (LLC)
- Corporation
- Partnership
- Nonprofit organization

The business must have a US physical address. P.O. boxes are not accepted.

## Identity information for each individual

For each individual we verify, we collect:

- Full legal name
- Date of birth
- Residential address
- Taxpayer identification number (TIN): a Social Security number (SSN) or an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN)
- An unexpired government-issued photo ID, such as a driver's license or passport

## Who we verify and what to provide, by business type

| Business type | Individuals we verify | Tax ID and documents |
|---|---|---|
| Sole proprietorship | The owner; no beneficial-ownership certification | SSN or EIN; a DBA ("doing business as") certificate if you trade under another name |
| LLC | Each beneficial owner (25% or more) plus one controlling person | EIN; articles of organization |
| Corporation | Each beneficial owner (25% or more) plus one controlling person | EIN; articles of incorporation |
| Partnership | Each beneficial owner (25% or more) plus one controlling person | EIN; partnership agreement |
| Nonprofit | The controlling person only, such as the executive director or treasurer | EIN; formation documents, such as articles of incorporation |

Every business must also provide a business license if its industry requires one.

## Beneficial owners and the controlling person

Legal entities (LLCs, corporations, and partnerships) must identify:

- **Beneficial owners:** each individual who directly or indirectly owns 25% or more of the business.
- **A controlling person:** one individual with significant responsibility to control, manage, or direct the business, such as a CEO, CFO, managing member, general partner, president, or treasurer.

If no individual owns 25% or more, we verify only the controlling person. One person can be both a beneficial owner and the controlling person. The individual opening the account certifies that the ownership information is accurate.

Sole proprietors do not complete a beneficial-ownership certification. For nonprofits, only the controlling person is verified.

## How long a decision takes

Most applications are decided within 1 business day. Applications that need additional review can take up to 5 business days, and we may contact you for more information or documents during that time. Our Onboarding team handles questions about an application in progress.

## Prohibited businesses

We cannot open accounts or process payments for the following businesses, and applications from them cannot be approved:

- Marijuana or cannabis businesses, including state-licensed dispensaries
- Cryptocurrency exchanges and crypto ATMs
- Money services businesses, such as money transmitters and check cashers
- Online gambling
- Adult entertainment

These businesses are prohibited even when they are licensed or legal under state law.

> **Note:** Sellers of hemp-derived CBD products are not prohibited. They are a restricted business type and need enhanced review (see below).

## Restricted businesses (enhanced review)

The following businesses may be approved only after enhanced due diligence:

- Sellers of hemp-derived CBD products
- Firearms and ammunition dealers
- Nutraceuticals or dietary supplements sold by subscription
- Travel agencies

Approval is not guaranteed. MerchantBridge support agents must not promise approval for a restricted business type and must route the applicant to the Onboarding team, which carries out the enhanced review and makes the decision.

## Keeping your ownership information current

We may periodically ask you to confirm or update your ownership information, including your beneficial owners and controlling person. Please also let us know when the ownership or control of your business changes.
