# Business Checking Plans
Effective: July 1, 2026 · Owner: Deposit Products

MerchantBridge offers three business checking plans: Bridge Essentials, Bridge Plus and Bridge Premier. Every plan includes the Bridge Business Debit Mastercard, Online Banking and the MBFS app, mobile deposit, ACH payments, wire transfers and merchant card processing. The plans differ in fee waivers, included transactions, payout timing and included services.

## Plan comparison

| Bridge Essentials | An average daily balance of at least $2,500, or at least $1,000 in MBFS card-processing deposits during the statement cycle | 150 | 2 business days after batch close | — |
| Bridge Plus | An average daily balance of at least $10,000 | 300 | Next business day after batch close | Standard ACH origination for the first 100 payments per statement cycle |

> **Note:** Fee amounts for each plan, including the monthly maintenance fee (shown on statements as MONTHLY SERVICE FEE) and the excess transaction fee, are listed in the Business Fee & Rate Schedule.

## Waiving the monthly maintenance fee

The monthly maintenance fee is waived for any statement cycle in which you meet your plan's waiver requirement:

- **Bridge Essentials:** an average daily balance of at least $2,500, or at least $1,000 in MBFS card-processing deposits (payouts of your card sales) during the statement cycle. Meeting either requirement waives the fee.
- **Bridge Plus:** an average daily balance of at least $10,000.
- **Bridge Premier:** a combined average daily balance of at least $50,000 across all of your MBFS business accounts, including Bridge Reserve Savings.

If you don't meet your plan's requirement during a statement cycle, your plan's monthly maintenance fee is charged for that cycle.

## Average daily balance and minimum daily balance

- **Average daily balance** is the sum of your end-of-day balances for each day in the statement cycle, divided by the number of days in the statement cycle. All three checking plan waivers use average daily balance.
- **Minimum daily balance** is the lowest end-of-day balance during the statement cycle. MBFS uses minimum daily balance only for the Bridge Reserve Savings monthly fee waiver (a minimum daily balance of $1,000). It is not used for checking plans.

> **Example:** A Bridge Essentials account ends 27 days of a 30-day statement cycle at $3,000 and the other 3 days at $200. Its average daily balance is (27 × $3,000 + 3 × $200) ÷ 30 = $2,720, which meets the $2,500 waiver requirement, even though its minimum daily balance for the cycle was $200.

## Included transactions

Each statement cycle includes 150 transactions on Bridge Essentials, 300 transactions on Bridge Plus and unlimited transactions on Bridge Premier. On Bridge Essentials and Bridge Plus, each transaction beyond the plan's allowance is charged the excess transaction fee (EXCESS ITEM FEE on your statement) shown in the Business Fee & Rate Schedule.

## Payout timing

Your plan sets how quickly standard payouts for card sales arrive after the daily batch closes at 8:00 PM ET: 2 business days after batch close on Bridge Essentials, and the next business day on Bridge Plus and Bridge Premier. For weekends, Federal Reserve holidays and Instant Payouts, see the Payouts and Settlement Policy.

## Interest

Bridge business checking accounts do not earn interest. To earn interest on idle balances, open a Bridge Reserve Savings account. A Bridge Reserve Savings account must be linked to an MBFS business checking account; see Bridge Reserve Savings Account Terms.

## Switching plans

You can upgrade or downgrade at any time, and switching plans is free.

1. Go to **Settings > Plan**.
2. Choose your new plan and confirm the change.

The new plan takes effect at the start of your next statement cycle. From that date, the new plan's fees, waiver requirement, included transactions and payout timing apply; your current plan stays in effect until then. For example, if you upgrade from Bridge Essentials to Bridge Plus, standard payouts change from 2 business days after batch close to the next business day, starting with your next statement cycle.
