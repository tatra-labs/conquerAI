# Account Closure and Dormant Accounts Policy
Effective: July 1, 2026 · Owner: Deposit Operations

This policy explains how to close your MerchantBridge business account, what happens to your balance, card processing, and terminals when you do, and when an inactive account becomes dormant.

## How to request a closure

An authorized signer on the account can request a closure:

- **By phone:** call Business Support at 1-800-555-0142 (Mon–Fri 7:00 AM–10:00 PM ET, Sat 9:00 AM–6:00 PM ET, closed Sunday).
- **By secure message:** in the MerchantBridge app or the Merchant Portal.

We verify the requester's identity before acting on the request. Employee cardholders and view-only users (such as a bookkeeper) cannot request a closure.

## Before you close: checklist

1. **Move your remaining balance** to another account your business uses.
2. **Let pending transactions clear,** including checks you have written, pending debit card purchases, and ACH payments.
3. **Cancel scheduled payments,** such as future-dated ACH payments and recurring transfers, and move vendor charges billed to your Bridge Business Debit Mastercard to another payment method.
4. **Redirect incoming payments** by giving anyone who pays you by ACH or wire your new account details.
5. **Issue any refunds you owe** customers from the Merchant Portal or your terminal.
6. **Include linked savings.** Bridge Reserve Savings must be linked to a MerchantBridge business checking account, so if you are closing your only checking account, move your savings balance too and include the savings account in your request.
7. **Download any statements** you want to keep.
8. **Gather any rented terminals** so you can return them on time (see below).

## What happens to your remaining balance

Any balance left at closure is sent to you by check to the address on file or to a verified external account, so make sure your address on file is current before you request the closure.

## Early closure fee

If the account is closed within 90 days of opening, an early account closure fee of $50.00 per account applies (fee code ACC-07 in the Business Fee & Rate Schedule; shown on statements as EARLY CLOSURE FEE).

## Card processing and final payouts

Closing your account ends card acceptance: you can no longer take card payments through MerchantBridge in person, online, or by invoice.

Chargebacks and refunds can still arise from sales made before the closure date. To cover them, MerchantBridge may retain a reserve from your final payouts for up to 120 days after closure. Reserved funds not needed for chargebacks or refunds are released to you when the reserve period ends. This closure reserve is separate from any rolling reserve under the Payouts and Settlement Policy.

## Returning BridgeTerminal hardware

| Terminal | If you rent it | If you bought it |
|---|---|---|
| BridgeTerminal Mini | Return within 30 days of closure using the prepaid return label, or a $199.00 non-return fee applies | No return needed |
| BridgeTerminal Pro | Return within 30 days of closure using the prepaid return label, or a $599.00 non-return fee applies | No return needed |

The terminal non-return fee is charged per terminal (fee codes HW-05 and HW-06; shown on statements as TERMINAL NONRETURN FEE). The same 30-day window applies if you cancel a terminal rental but keep your account open.

> **Note:** Purchased terminals are yours to keep, but they cannot accept card payments through MerchantBridge after your account is closed.

## Closed accounts cannot be reopened

A closed account cannot be reopened. To bank or process payments with MerchantBridge again, submit a new application; it is reviewed under the Account Opening and Identity Verification Policy.

## Dormant accounts and escheatment

- **When an account becomes dormant:** after 12 consecutive months with no customer-initiated activity, such as a deposit, withdrawal, transfer, or payment that you make. Fees we charge to the account do not count as activity.
- **Dormant account fee:** $5.00 per month while the account is dormant (fee code ACC-08; shown on statements as DORMANT ACCT FEE).
- **Escheatment:** if the account stays inactive through the dormancy period set by state law (typically 3–5 years, depending on the state), the funds must be transferred to the state as unclaimed property. You would then claim them from the state's unclaimed property program, not from MerchantBridge.

To avoid the dormant account fee, make at least one customer-initiated transaction every 12 months, or close accounts you no longer need. If your account is already dormant, contact Business Support to keep using it.
