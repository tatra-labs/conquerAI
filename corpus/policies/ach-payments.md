# ACH Payments Policy
Effective: July 1, 2026 · Owner: Payments Operations

This policy explains how ACH payments work with your MerchantBridge business checking account: processing windows, authorization, returned payments in both directions, unauthorized debits, payments presented without enough funds, and stop payments.

## ACH processing windows

| Option | Submit by (on a business day) | Settles |
|---|---|---|
| Standard ACH | 7:00 PM ET | In 1–2 business days |
| Same-day ACH | 2:45 PM ET | The same business day |

Same-day ACH is available for payments of up to $1,000,000 each. A payment submitted after its cut-off is treated as submitted on the next business day. Incoming ACH credits are available the same business day they are received.

## Authorization for ACH debits

You must have the payer's authorization before you originate an ACH debit from their account. Keep a record of each authorization in case the payment is disputed later.

## Returned ACH payments

An ACH payment you originated can be returned in either direction:

- **An ACH debit you originated** (you pulled funds from a customer's account) is returned — for example, because of insufficient funds or a closed account. MerchantBridge reverses the credit we gave you for that payment and charges the ACH return fee.
- **An ACH credit you sent** (for example, a payment to a vendor whose account is closed) is returned. The funds come back to your account, and the ACH return fee applies.

Consumer payers can return your debits as unauthorized for up to 60 days (return code R10), so debits from consumer accounts carry a longer return risk.

## Unauthorized ACH debits on your business account

If an ACH debit you didn't authorize posts to your MerchantBridge account, notify us **within 1 business day of the posting** by calling Business Support at 1-800-555-0142. This lets us return it within the ACH network's 2-banking-day window for business accounts (return code R29). Later claims may not be recoverable.

> **Note:** Network rules give a consumer up to 60 days to return an unauthorized debit, but give a business account only 2 banking days. That is why a consumer customer can return your debit long after you've been paid, while you need to report an unauthorized debit on your own business account within 1 business day.

Reviewing your account activity every business day helps you catch unauthorized debits in time.

## Returned items (NSF): payments presented without enough funds

If a payment from your account, such as an ACH debit or a check you wrote, is presented without enough funds to cover it, MerchantBridge returns it unpaid and charges the returned item (NSF) fee. We charge a maximum of 3 returned item fees per business day. For example, if five items are returned on the same business day, you are charged for three.

## Stop payments

Place a stop payment in Online Banking or by phone with Business Support.

- **ACH debits:** place the request at least **3 business days** before the scheduled debit. We also recommend asking the company to cancel your authorization.
- **Checks:** a check stop payment remains in effect for **6 months** and can be renewed.
