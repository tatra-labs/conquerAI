# Payouts and Settlement Policy
Effective: July 1, 2026 · Owner: Merchant Operations

This policy explains how MerchantBridge groups your card sales into daily batches, when standard payouts arrive, how Instant Payouts work, and when payouts may be held.

## Daily batch close

Card transactions are grouped into a daily batch that closes automatically at 8:00 PM ET. Sales made after 8:00 PM ET are included in the next day's batch. Each closed batch is paid out as a standard payout on the schedule set by your business checking plan.

## Standard payout timing by plan

- **Bridge Essentials:** standard payouts arrive 2 business days after batch close.
- **Bridge Plus:** standard payouts arrive the next business day after batch close.
- **Bridge Premier:** standard payouts arrive the next business day after batch close.

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding Federal Reserve holidays.

## Weekends and Federal Reserve holidays

Standard payouts are not initiated on weekends or Federal Reserve holidays. Batches that close on Saturday or Sunday are treated as closing on Monday.

> **Example:** On Bridge Essentials, batches that close on Saturday and Sunday are treated as closing on Monday, so they arrive on Wednesday, 2 business days after Monday. On Bridge Plus or Bridge Premier, the same weekend batches arrive on Tuesday.

When no Federal Reserve holiday falls between the batch close and the payout, standard payouts arrive as follows:

| Batch closes (8:00 PM ET) | Bridge Essentials payout arrives | Bridge Plus and Bridge Premier payout arrives |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Wednesday | Tuesday |
| Tuesday | Thursday | Wednesday |
| Wednesday | Friday | Thursday |
| Thursday | Monday | Friday |
| Friday | Tuesday | Monday |
| Saturday or Sunday (treated as Monday) | Wednesday | Tuesday |

Federal Reserve holidays are not business days, so they are skipped when counting business days to a payout. For example, a Bridge Plus batch that closes on the Friday before a Monday holiday arrives on Tuesday.

## First payout for new merchants

Your first payout may take up to 5 business days while we verify your account. After that, payouts follow your plan's standard timing.

## Instant Payouts

An Instant Payout sends funds from your card sales to an eligible debit card in your business's name, so you don't have to wait for the next standard payout.

- Instant Payouts are available 24/7, including weekends and Federal Reserve holidays.
- Instant Payouts usually arrive within 30 minutes.
- The maximum is $10,000 per Instant Payout and $25,000 per day.
- Each Instant Payout is charged your plan's Instant Payout fee (INSTANT PAYOUT FEE on your statement), as listed in the Business Fee & Rate Schedule.

## Deductions from payouts

Refunds you issue are deducted from your next payout. When MBFS receives a chargeback, the disputed amount plus the chargeback fee is also deducted from your next payout. If your payouts aren't enough to cover these amounts, they are debited from your checking account instead.

## Payout holds and rolling reserves

If MBFS sees elevated chargeback or refund risk on your account, we may hold payouts or set a rolling reserve. A rolling reserve withholds up to 10% of your daily card volume, and each day's withheld amount is held for up to 90 days before it is released to you.

## Payout account changes

For your security, a change to the bank account that receives your payouts must be approved in the MBFS app by an owner or admin on your account. MBFS can't make this change over the phone.

## Changing plans

You can switch plans at any time in Settings > Plan, and switching is free. The new plan's payout timing and fees take effect at the start of your next statement cycle. For example, if you move from Bridge Essentials to Bridge Plus, standard payouts change from 2 business days after batch close to the next business day, starting with your next statement cycle.
