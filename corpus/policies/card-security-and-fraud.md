# Debit Card Security and Fraud Policy
Effective: January 15, 2026 · Owner: Fraud Prevention

This policy applies to every Bridge Business Debit Mastercard on your account, including employee cards.

## Lock or unlock your card

If your card is misplaced, lock it instantly in the MerchantBridge app under **Cards > Lock card**. While the card is locked, new purchases and ATM withdrawals are declined. Unlocking it from the same screen restores the card, and the card number stays the same. If the card is lost or stolen, or you see transactions you don't recognize, also report it.

## Report a lost, stolen, or compromised card

Report your card immediately if it is lost or stolen, or if you think someone else has your card details:

- **In the app:** go to **Cards > Report a problem**.
- **By phone:** call the 24/7 Card Fraud line at **1-800-555-0199**.

Reporting cancels the card, and we issue a replacement with a new card number. A reported card can't be unlocked or used again, even if you find it later. Update any recurring payments that used the old card number.

## Replacement cards

Choose standard or expedited delivery when you report the card.

| Delivery option | Arrives in | Bridge Essentials | Bridge Plus | Bridge Premier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard replacement | 5–7 business days | Free | Free | Free |
| Expedited replacement | 2 business days | $25.00 | $25.00 | Free |

Add the new card to a digital wallet such as Apple Pay or Google Pay right away, before the plastic card arrives.

## Disputing unauthorized card transactions

If you find a card transaction you didn't authorize, lock or report your card, then dispute the transaction within **60 days** of the statement date on which it first appeared. You can dispute in the app (**Cards > Report a problem**) or by calling the 24/7 Card Fraud line at 1-800-555-0199. Have the date, amount, and merchant name ready.

Provisional credit for eligible claims is typically issued within **10 business days** while we investigate. If we find that a transaction was authorized, we reverse the provisional credit and let you know.

These timelines are MerchantBridge policy. Federal Regulation E consumer protections do not apply to business accounts.

If one of your customers disputes a card payment your business accepted, see the Chargebacks and Disputes Policy instead. For an ACH debit you didn't authorize, notify us within 1 business day of posting; see the ACH Payments Policy.

## Mastercard Zero Liability

Mastercard Zero Liability protection applies to unauthorized purchases made with your card that you report promptly. Conditions apply.

## Card skimming

A skimmer is a device that criminals attach to a card reader to copy card details when a card is swiped or inserted. Skimmers are most often found at gas station pumps and standalone ATMs. To lower your risk, use tap-to-pay whenever you can, avoid card readers that look loose or damaged, and cover the keypad when you enter your PIN.

If you think your card was skimmed:

1. Lock the card in the app (**Cards > Lock card**).
2. Report it and request a replacement in the app (**Cards > Report a problem**) or by calling the 24/7 Card Fraud line at **1-800-555-0199**.
3. Review your recent transactions and dispute any you don't recognize within 60 days of the statement date on which they first appeared.

## Overpayment scams

Overpayment scams target businesses that sell goods or services. A buyer "accidentally" pays more than they owe, often with a cashier's check or a stolen card, and asks you to send the difference back by wire or gift card. The original payment later turns out to be fraudulent and is reversed, and the money you sent is usually lost. Be wary of any buyer who pressures you to act quickly or wants the money sent to someone else, such as a "shipping agent".

To protect your business:

- Do not send money back until the original payment has fully cleared and can no longer be reversed. Funds from a deposited check can become available before the check has finished clearing.
- Wires are final once released, and a recall is not guaranteed to return the funds.
- Contact our Fraud team at 1-800-555-0199, 24/7, before you send anything.

## How we contact you

Scammers sometimes pretend to be MerchantBridge, but we will never ask for your PIN, full card number, password, or one-time passcode, whether by phone, text, or email. If someone asks, hang up and call the 24/7 Card Fraud line at 1-800-555-0199. If you have already shared these details, lock your card and call us right away. Forward suspicious emails to phishing@merchantbridge.example.
