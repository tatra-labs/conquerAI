# Notice of Fee Changes Effective January 1, 2027
Published: October 1, 2026 · Effective: January 1, 2027 · Owner: Pricing

We are updating three fees on the Business Fee & Rate Schedule. The changes take effect on January 1, 2027. Until then, the current Business Fee & Rate Schedule (effective July 1, 2026) continues to apply.

## What's changing on January 1, 2027

| Code | Service | Plan | Current fee | New fee (effective January 1, 2027) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ACC-01 | Monthly maintenance fee | Bridge Essentials | $12.00 | $15.00 |
| MER-04 | Instant Payout | Bridge Essentials | 1.5% of payout (min $0.50) | 1.75% of payout (min $0.50) |
| MER-04 | Instant Payout | Bridge Plus | 1.5% of payout (min $0.50) | 1.75% of payout (min $0.50) |
| ACC-03 | Paper statements | Bridge Essentials | $3.00 | $4.00 |
| ACC-03 | Paper statements | Bridge Plus | $3.00 | $4.00 |

- **Bridge Essentials monthly maintenance fee:** increases from $12.00 to $15.00 per month. The waiver requirements are not changing.
- **Instant Payout fee on Bridge Essentials and Bridge Plus:** increases from 1.5% to 1.75% of each payout. The $0.50 minimum per payout is unchanged. For example, a $500 Instant Payout currently costs $7.50 and will cost $8.75 from January 1, 2027.
- **Paper statements on Bridge Essentials and Bridge Plus:** increase from $3.00 to $4.00 per month. e-Statements remain free on all plans.

If your business is on Bridge Premier, none of these changes apply to your account.

## What's not changing

The January 1, 2027 changes do not affect:

- Wire transfer fees, domestic or international, incoming or outgoing.
- The chargeback fee, which remains $25.00 per chargeback.
- Card-processing rates for card-present and card-not-present transactions.
- The Bridge Premier Instant Payout fee, which remains 1.0% of payout (min $0.50).
- Paper statements on Bridge Premier, which remain free.
- Monthly maintenance fee waiver requirements on any plan, and the Bridge Plus and Bridge Premier monthly maintenance fees.

Standard payouts continue on your plan's usual schedule and are not affected by the Instant Payout change. Other fees on the Business Fee & Rate Schedule are not affected by this notice.

## How to avoid the Bridge Essentials increase

1. **Meet the waiver.** In any statement cycle where you keep a $2,500 average daily balance, or receive at least $1,000 in MerchantBridge card-processing deposits, the Bridge Essentials monthly maintenance fee is waived, both before and after January 1, 2027.
2. **Switch plans.** You can change plans at any time in Settings > Plan, and switching is free. The new plan, including its fees and payout timing, takes effect at the start of your next statement cycle. See Business Checking Plans to compare plans.

To avoid the paper statement fee on Bridge Essentials or Bridge Plus, switch to e-Statements, which are free on all plans.

## How we notified customers

This notice was published on October 1, 2026, and we emailed it to customers at least 30 days before the changes take effect. You don't need to do anything to keep your current plan and services.
