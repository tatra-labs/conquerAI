# Held-out v2 labeling notes

Recorded by the two blind labelers while writing `heldout_v2.toml`. They are not used for scoring; they say
where the gold involved a judgment call, so a reviewer can disagree with the label rather than with the system.

- Checked by script: tomllib parses the file, all 84 supports are verbatim substrings of their document (or real 2026-07 rows; WIR-01 Essentials is $30.00), every regex compiles, and no support uses the superseded fee-schedule-2025-01. (The local path of the labeler's working file was removed before publishing; its labels are merged into `heldout_v2.toml`.)
- V02: I read 'this morning' as the as-of date, which is a Monday. A Saturday batch counts as closing Monday and pays out Tuesday or Wednesday, so this morning's payout cannot be Saturday's sales; that is why weekend_batch is required. I left 'refuse' out of acceptable even though the exact deduction needs account data; add it if a pure 'can't see the account' reply should pass.
- V02: Not made required: processing fees and a rolling reserve. That payouts are net of processing fees is only implied, by the 1099-K wording in help-tax-forms. A 10% rolling reserve would come to exactly $300 on $3,000 of sales.
- V04: The question is ambiguous. The 15-day non-return-fee reversal rule (internal agent-handling-guidelines) is required on the reading that the fee may already have posted. Whether a bought terminal can be refunded is not covered anywhere, so the label is 'partial'.
- V09: I counted notice-2027-fee-changes (marked upcoming) as a support for the plan-switch rule, because it repeats current policy (next statement cycle, switching is free). Remove those two supports if an upcoming document must never be a support.
- V10: The knowledge base disagrees on how long to keep a deposited check: funds-availability (policy) says 14 days, help-mobile-deposit says 30. I forbade days:30 by authority order, which will also flag an answer that mentions both numbers.
- Forbidden wrong-plan and wrong-service amounts can flag answers that add correct comparison details: V06 ($25 Plus, $50 international, $15 incoming) and V03 ($5,000 mobile deposit).
- Regex matchers are used where exact text was too brittle. V05 view_only_scope also accepts 'balances', 'statements' or 'transaction history', and V09 switching_free is just 'text:free', so both are loose; tighten them if they credit too easily.
- V01 is labeled refuse with no facts. The only relevant line in the knowledge base is 'MBFS cannot provide tax advice. Please consult a qualified tax professional.', which appears in help-tax-forms and business-savings.
- Checked by script: the TOML parses, every quote is a verbatim substring of its doc block, every fee-schedule row and column exists, and no superseded doc is cited. (The local paths of the working file and the validator script were removed before publishing; the labels are merged into `heldout_v2.toml`, and `tests/test_dataset.py` re-checks every quote, row and column.)
- V19 accepts only clarify and has no required facts. I wrote 'facts = []' so the key exists; delete that line if the loader rejects an empty array. An answer that lists every plan-based daily limit and says the KB has no separate limit for new accounts could arguably also count as answer or partial.
- V12 has four prices, but each item allows at most 3 facts. The two rental prices ($15 Mini, $39 Pro) share one regex fact, which an answer satisfies by giving either rental price.
- Four facts use regex matchers (V12 rental, V13 agents_cannot_reverse, V16 change_password, V20 no_tax_advice). The V13 regex is a rough negation detector: it can match a wrong 'yes' answer whose sentence contains a negation near 'refund', and it misses answers that say only 'Only Merchant Risk can reverse it'.
- V11 waiver uses money:10000, which assumes the money matcher reads '$10,000' as 10000. Its forbidden $12/$45 (wrong-plan fees) would also flag a correct answer that suggests switching plans and quotes those fees.
- V16 never_asks uses the generic text:never and relies on the citation check to limit it to the right sentence. fraud_line requires 1-800-555-0199, so an answer that gives only the Business Support number (help-online-banking-security also allows that) misses the fact.
- V14 accepts only partial, not a bare refuse. 2026-10-19 is a Monday, so 'yesterday afternoon' was a Sunday, when wires are not processed; I did not make that a fact. Requiring the 4:00 PM domestic cut-off assumes the supplier wire is domestic.
- V18 also accepts partial because the KB describes extra debit cards only as employee cards. It does not say whether a co-owner can get an owner-level card. The rule that user changes can't be made over the phone is in the notes, not a required fact.
- V20 is a refuse item but has one fact (MBFS cannot provide tax advice / consult a qualified tax professional). Drop it if refuse items should have no facts.
- V13 keeps 'completing the SAQ stops future monthly charges' in the notes rather than as a required fact.
- Outside this batch: the corpus disagrees on how long to keep a mobile-deposited check. The funds-availability policy says 14 days; the help-mobile-deposit article says 30.
